## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee sales data and transforming it into an interactive Excel dashboard. The dashboard provides a clear view of revenue performance, customer payment preferences, monthly sales trends, and purchasing patterns throughout the day.

The goal was to turn raw sales data into meaningful insights through data analysis and visualization.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue across different coffee types.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Identify monthly revenue trends.
- Explore the number of cups sold by hour of day.
- Present key performance indicators in a single interactive dashboard.
- Communicate findings through visualizations and business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis and dashboard development.
- **Pivot Tables:** Summarizing and analyzing sales data.
- **Pivot Charts:** Visualizing revenue and sales patterns.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filtering data by coffee type, payment type, year, and month.
- **KPI Cards:** Displaying important performance metrics.
- **Claude AI:** Assisting with visual formatting, layout, alignment, and dashboard presentation.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

**1. Best-Selling Coffee by Revenue**

Latte generated the highest revenue at 27,866, followed by Americano with Milk at 25,269. Together, these two coffee types contributed approximately 46% of total revenue.

**2. Customer Payment Preferences**

Card payments accounted for 97.2% of revenue, while cash payments contributed 2.8%. This indicates a strong preference for card-based payments in the analyzed dataset.

**3. Sales Trends Throughout the Day**

Sales peaked around 10 AM, with 349 cups sold. The dashboard also highlights changes in hourly demand, helping identify periods of higher customer activity.

**4. Monthly Revenue Analysis**

The monthly revenue chart makes it easier to compare performance across months and identify periods of higher and lower revenue.

## 🤖 My Approach to AI-Assisted Productivity

**I didn't let AI do my work. I let it make my work faster.**

I built the dashboard manually in Excel, including the Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, KPI cards, and analysis.

I used Claude AI to improve the dashboard's visual formatting and presentation. This helped me complete the overall dashboard in approximately **2 hours**, while keeping control over the analytical work and final output.

This project reflects my approach to using AI as a productivity tool: understanding the work, knowing how to validate it, and using AI where it adds value.
